04 / Release signal

Listen → respond → decide.

The eventual interaction layer is intentionally simple: listen to a track, back it once, and optionally say why. Those signals can help decide whether a song stays private, moves to SoundCloud for a public experiment, or enters the full DSP release workflow.

Backing a track is a signal, not an automatic release vote. The final decision remains part of the creative process.

02INSPIRATION / PRIOR ART

A QUESTION THAT DIDN’T GO AWAY.

If the computer can’t be accountable, where does accountability live?

AGENT→AUTHORITY→BOUNDARY→EVIDENCE→HUMAN

That is increasingly the through-line behind LEDGATo and Khrystal: not making the model “responsible,” but making delegated authority explicit, enforceable, observable, and attributable to people.

This is inspiration, not validation. A historical warning can sharpen the question; the build still has to supply the proof.

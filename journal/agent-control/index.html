AGENT CONTROL / CURRENT ARCHITECTURE

Intelligence is not authority.

A working map of the system emerging underneath the builds: who decides what should happen, who may authorize it, who executes it, and how reality is independently checked afterward.

STATUS: architecture and bounded proofs exist. Universal non-bypassable enforcement and independent verification across every harness do not yet.

03 / PRINCIPLE

The trust boundary.

The architecture becomes useful when claims by intelligent actors are treated as proposals, not facts.

Intelligence ≠ AuthorityAn agent can reason about an action without possessing the right to perform it.
Authority ≠ ExecutionA grant authorizes a bounded action; it does not prove the action occurred correctly.
Execution ≠ EvidenceThe actor saying “done” is not proof. External state and independent verification close the loop.

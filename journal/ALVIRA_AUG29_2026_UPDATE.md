# ALVIRA — Aug. 29, 2026 product-decision update

## Why this entry exists

ALVIRA changed materially in one build session. The product moved from a collection of related surfaces toward one clearer Context Intelligence system, and the public explanation was simplified so someone new to AI can understand why it matters before learning the category language.

## What changed

### The product is the maintained Context loop

The working model is now:

**Capture → Understand → Reflect → Update → Reuse**

The interview is a way to begin and deepen Context, not the product itself. Context Mirror / Live Context makes that understanding visible while it forms. History shows how it changes. Reuse and Bridge are ways to put maintained Context to work.

### Reflect is no longer a separate commercial product

ALVIRA Reflect is now treated as part of the core Context loop: the place where a person revisits, validates, challenges, and deepens what ALVIRA understands.

The older standalone **Reflect Build** and **Reflect Care** pricing model was retired from the public product architecture. Existing `meos_build` access remains a compatibility path for earlier entitlements rather than the new commercial boundary.

Current intent:

- Free: basic Reflect within the Free Context/interview limits.
- Pro: full ongoing Reflect across multiple Contexts and continuous updates.
- Lifetime: core Reflect included with the covered ALVIRA Context capabilities.
- Founding Beta: full customer-facing Reflect access.

### Pricing was simplified around one ALVIRA system

The pricing page now centers Free, Pro, Lifetime, and the limited Founding Beta path. It no longer asks a new visitor to understand a separate Reflect purchase before they understand ALVIRA itself.

The commercial principle is simple: do not charge separately for the parts required to complete the basic Context Intelligence loop. Separate add-ons should exist only when they represent genuinely incremental cost or value.

### Founding Beta became a real validation cohort

Founding Beta is intentionally not a newsletter list or a generic free-software giveaway.

Selected testers receive complimentary unlimited access to ALVIRA's customer-facing product capabilities under the approved account for the life of the ALVIRA service. In return, the beta asks for genuine product use and candid feedback about workflow, UI, relevance, effectiveness, confusion, and failures.

Applications are limited and reviewed individually because the goal is to learn closely from real usage rather than maximize signups.

### Public positioning became much simpler

The public education layer now starts from the visitor's question:

**Why ALVIRA for me?**

The plain-language idea:

> AI can do more for you when it knows more about you.

Most AI starts with what a person types into the current box. ALVIRA helps build and maintain the background that should already be available before AI answers, writes, plans, or acts with someone.

Use cases are now framed for personal life, founders/business owners, creatives, professionals, and teams rather than assuming a visitor already understands Context Intelligence.

### Ownership became a first-class product promise

A second public idea now sits beside continuity:

> **Your Context should belong to you.**

ALVIRA can maintain Context without owning it.

This revives the encrypted-dossier idea that existed at the beginning of MeOS and gives it a clearer role inside ALVIRA rather than turning it into another product SKU.

The intended architecture is:

- **Context** — what ALVIRA knows.
- **Reflect** — where that understanding is examined and evolved.
- **History** — how the understanding changed.
- **Dossier** — the private, portable representation the user controls.
- **Reuse / Bridge** — where the user chooses to put that Context to work.

The full encrypted Dossier, restore/import, and richer portability remain forthcoming and should be labeled as such until shipped. Security itself is not a premium feature; future paid Dossier value would concern richer portability, recovery, history, and control.

## The MeOS through-line

The original idea should not disappear from the archive:

> **MeOS began as the dossier. ALVIRA became the system that keeps the dossier alive.**

That evolution matters because the product did not begin as a prompt utility. It began with the idea that a person could hold a durable, private representation of themselves that intelligence could work from. ALVIRA became the maintenance, reflection, history, and reuse system needed to make that object useful over time.

## What is proven vs. still a bet

Working product surfaces now include the interview flow, saved Context, continuous-update direction, Context Mirror, initial Context History, Reuse, exports, Founding Beta instrumentation/application, and the simplified public/pricing architecture.

Still to prove:

- whether people return often enough to maintain Context over time;
- whether cross-session continuity materially improves AI usefulness;
- whether Reflect surfaces useful changes rather than merely summarizing prior answers;
- whether users understand and value ownership/portability enough for Dossier to become a meaningful differentiator;
- whether Founding Beta testers generate enough real-world evidence to sharpen the product for broader users.

## Current evidence target

The next meaningful proof is no longer "can ALVIRA build a profile?"

It is:

**Does maintained, inspectable, user-owned Context make AI measurably more useful to a person over repeated real work?**

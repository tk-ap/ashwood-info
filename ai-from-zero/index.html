NO AI BACKGROUND REQUIRED

Start with something you already do.

AI is useful when it makes a real task easier, clearer, or faster. You do not need to learn the whole industry first.

Find your entry point ↓
01START SMALL

Use the lightest form of AI that helps.

Bring the thing you already care about. Let complexity earn its way in.

UNDERSTAND

Explain something confusing.

COMPARE

Show the tradeoffs between options.

ORGANIZE

Turn scattered material into a plan.

CREATE

Help draft, shape, or transform something.

MONITOR

Watch for a change and tell you when it matters.

Simple rule: ask → add context → reuse what works → automate only when repetition becomes a real burden.

02WHEN AN AGENT HELPS

An agent is useful when the work has steps.

If a bounded process keeps repeating, AI can sometimes carry more of it for you.

ASSISTANT

Helps with the next answer.

You ask. It responds. You decide what happens next.

AGENT

Can carry a goal through steps.

You give it an outcome, context, boundaries, and tools. It works until it needs your judgment.

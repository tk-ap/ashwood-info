DE-JARGON THE BUILD / USE THIS WHILE YOU WORK

What does that word actually mean?

Technical language should not be the price of admission. Search the words you encounter while building with AI, understand them in plain English, see what they look like, and connect them back to the actual ASHWOOD builds.

01THE MENTAL MODEL

Six things people constantly mix together.

You do not need perfect definitions before you start. You do need a rough map of what role each layer plays.

MODELThe intelligence.

The system that can interpret and generate language, code, images, or other outputs.

AGENTThe worker.

A model given a goal, context, tools, and some ability to take multiple steps.

WORKFLOWThe path.

The sequence of steps the work should follow.

HARNESSThe workplace.

The surrounding system that gives an agent tools, instructions, memory, checks, and a place to run.

WORKFORCEThe team.

Multiple agents or harnesses coordinated toward larger work.

GOVERNANCEThe boundaries.

The rules for what machines may do, when humans must decide, and what evidence must remain.

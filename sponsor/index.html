ASHWOOD sponsorship is for people and organizations who want to put resources behind something I am already making — a project, experiment, release, research step, or build milestone. It is not a creative-services menu.

01How it works

Choose a piece of work you care about, or tell me the area you want to support. Before anything is accepted, I define the bounded scope, what the money unlocks, what completion means, and what evidence closes the loop.

The work remains independent. Sponsorship does not automatically grant ownership, editorial approval, product control, IP rights, or guaranteed exposure.

02What support can unlock

Build milestone — infrastructure, testing, research, or another bounded step needed to move an existing ASHWOOD project forward.

Creative release — production costs around music, visual art, writing, photography, or another release already within the practice.

Public experiment — a specific question worth testing and documenting through the Build Journal or another appropriate ASHWOOD surface.

Available sponsorships and costs should be tied to the actual next milestone rather than a generic rate card.

03What a sponsor receives

Acknowledgment agreed before acceptance; a direct update when the sponsored milestone closes; a clear record of what the support enabled; and any additional project-specific access or artifact stated in the scope.

No guaranteed views, impressions, conversions, or favorable outcome.

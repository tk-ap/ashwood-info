ASHWOOD sponsorship is for people and organizations who want to put resources behind something I am already making — a project, experiment, release, research step, or build milestone. It is not a creative-services menu.

01How it works

Choose a piece of work you care about, or tell me the area you want to support. Before anything is accepted, I define the bounded scope, what the money unlocks, what completion means, and what evidence closes the loop.

The work remains independent. Sponsorship does not automatically grant ownership, editorial approval, product control, IP rights, or guaranteed exposure.

02Current sponsor targets

AgentOS / governed workforce proof — fund the bounded infrastructure and test costs required to prove an independent recovery/execution objective can complete with evidence without recreating equivalent founder coordination overhead.

LEDGATo / enforcement proof — fund the external test infrastructure needed to close remaining proof gates around restart behavior, fail-closed behavior, and independent end-to-end verification.

ASHWOOD / creative releases — fund a named production cost tied to music, visual art, photography, writing, or another release already in motion. Each sponsorship names the artifact before money is accepted.

Public experiments / Build Journal — fund a bounded research or testing cost around a question already being documented publicly, with the resulting evidence preserved whether the hypothesis succeeds or fails.

Not a sponsorship target: building ALVIRA itself. ALVIRA already exists; its current proof burden is whether persistent Context creates measurable value. Individuals can participate through Founding Beta and organizations through the separate Design Partner / POC path.

Support amounts are set from the actual next milestone and its real external cost — not from a generic sponsorship rate card.

03What a sponsor receives

Acknowledgment agreed before acceptance; a direct update when the sponsored milestone closes; a clear record of what the support enabled; and any additional project-specific access or artifact stated in the scope.

No guaranteed views, impressions, conversions, or favorable outcome.

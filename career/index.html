03 / How I work

Build. Learn. Understand. Improve.

01

Build against a real problem.

Start with an operating constraint, not an abstract exercise. The work exposes what I actually need to learn.

02

Learn at the constraint.

Research the environment, people, data, dependencies, failure conditions, and concepts the build makes necessary.

03

Make the system understandable.

Trace why it works, where authority lives, what assumptions it depends on, and what evidence supports the result.

04

Improve from evidence.

Turn what the build taught me into a more durable system, clearer decision, or better next experiment.

03ALVIRA Founding Beta

Limited testing cohort / applications reviewed

Don’t just follow
the build. Test it.

ALVIRA is opening a small number of Founding Beta accounts for people who genuinely want to use the product and help answer the question behind the build: does maintained context make AI meaningfully more useful to you over time?

What selected testers get: complimentary unlimited access to ALVIRA’s customer-facing product capabilities under the account granted the Founding Beta entitlement. That access stays with the account for the life of the ALVIRA service.

What the beta asks in return: genuine product use and candid feedback on the workflow, UI, relevance, effectiveness, confusion, and failures you encounter. There is no required quota of reports; the point is useful evidence from real use.

Why the cohort is limited: I want to learn closely from each tester rather than collect a large list of people who only want free software. Applying does not automatically create an account or grant access.

04Creative partnerships

Brands · culture · creative technology

Make something
specific together.

ASHWOOD works with aligned partners on bounded creative projects across image, sound, writing, technology, and the spaces between them. The offer is the work itself — not a promise of audience size or guaranteed impressions.

Creative partner: an original campaign concept, creative treatment, editorial artifact, or agreed social deliverable built around a real fit between the brand and the practice.

Build sponsor: support a specific public experiment, release, research step, or build artifact and receive an agreed acknowledgment plus a completion/evidence update.

Working minimums: bounded collaborations generally start around $500; sponsored editorial/build artifacts around $750; multi-deliverable partnerships around $1,500. Larger production, licensing, paid usage, exclusivity, or extended campaigns are scoped separately.

Standard short-project terms are 50% to begin and 50% on agreed completion, with production expenses approved separately. One revision round is included unless the scope says otherwise. Usage rights, paid media, exclusivity, and licensing are explicit rather than assumed.

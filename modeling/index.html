05 / AboutThey / He

Model and muse.

TK Ashwood is a non-binary model and recording artist based in Downtown Los Angeles, working across fashion, editorial, runway, and creative portraiture. They also release music as t.kap.

They are especially drawn to projects that celebrate individuality, strong visual storytelling, and inclusive creative expression. TK is available for select bookings and travel.

Represented by WHO Management Agency. Direct bookings and select collaborations are also welcome. Beyond modeling and music, TK is building an emerging technology and AI ecosystem. Follow the work in BUILDS ↗, or visit ALVIRA ↗.

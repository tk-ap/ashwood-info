## The cuts that most affect the book

Removing these six changes the archive the most — the first two are hard cuts, the rest are the duplicate WEAKs whose absence tightens everything around them:

1. **F07 (LA Fashion Week)** — the only frame in 57 with no face at all: your back, walking away, while another model faces the camera. It actively argues against the book's premise.
2. **P31 (Studio streetwear)** — the second copy of the weakest setup in the archive (glasses, chair, upturned slack face). Keeping it makes P29 look like a theme instead of an accident.
3. **F02 (ALIVE)** — the duplicate backstage smile. One smile this good is a highlight; two in a row makes it look like the only candid that worked.
4. **P15 (Denim)** — the softer copy of P11's stool shot. Side by side, it makes the stronger frame look accidental.
5. **F06 (LA Fashion Week)** — the duplicate locker frontal. The locker set is one of the archive's best sequences at three frames; at four, it pads.
6. **P05 (Tailoring)** — the downcast copy of the opening brocade portrait. It sits second in the whole archive's first story, and it spends the opener's momentum on a face looking at the floor.

**Bottom line:** 36 keeps is a real book. The archive doesn't have a face problem — it has a repetition problem. Cut the doubles, and nearly every remaining frame has its eyes on the camera.

# Applying Ownership and Stewardship in ASHWOOD

Status: owner-requested guidance, 2026-10-08; effective after merge and fresh instruction loading. No public design change or background automation is introduced by this document.

Read the shared [AgentOS ownership, stewardship and durable-value policy](https://github.com/tk-ap/agent-os/blob/main/policies/OWNERSHIP_STEWARDSHIP_AND_DURABLE_VALUE.md) before material product, creative, growth, partnership or continuity decisions. The companion AgentOS PR must be merged before this guide is treated as active. If the shared policy is unavailable, report that limitation and retain existing authorization/privacy rules.

The reference is a documentary about Nipsey Hussle's work and Blacc Sam's stewardship. Apply its themes to TK's own goals and identity; do not imitate their brands or imply endorsement. The shared policy owns interpretation and authority limits; this guide owns ASHWOOD-specific application.

## Public portfolio and writing

- Preserve TK's authorship, creative originals, rights, credits and distinct modeling/music/acting/poetry identity.
- Make work and its real value understandable. Avoid unsupported prestige, borrowed slogans and reach-driven claims.
- Keep creative Portfolio, writing and Build Journal responsibilities distinct. Sustained creative practice is valuable even when it is not a growth experiment.
- Respect the existing location/privacy boundary and asset-fidelity rules. Stewardship is not permission to expose private material.

## Build Journal

Use the existing entry and evidence model to explain what was attempted, learned, changed and actually verified. When relevant, explain the lasting asset or capability created and the unresolved tradeoff. Preserve failures and uncertainty; do not rewrite the history as inevitable success.

Use existing durable write paths and record identities. Do not create a parallel archive, invent source evidence, or infer that a merged PR is a deployed capability.

## Private Workspace and cockpit

- Surface the few actions that improve TK's next decision: verified changes, real blockers, time-sensitive opportunities and actual owner decisions.
- Preserve canonical owners for task state, product evidence, recommendations and approvals; Workspace remains a projection and interaction surface.
- Optimize for useful outcomes and less repeated administration. Avoid decorative activity, noisy reminders and extra dashboards without a demonstrated benefit.
- Career, auditions and Funding should support practical livelihood and creative goals. Preserve observed vs inferred application/submission states and existing eligibility/evidence gates.
- Retain continuity through existing records and handoffs, so another worker can resume the work without requiring TK to reconstruct it. Do not claim unattended monitoring unless observed.

## Build and growth decisions

Before materially expanding a feature, audience channel or provider dependency, apply the shared policy's relevant decision questions in the existing task or experiment record. Start with a small useful proof, inspect quality and economics, preserve control and record the learning. Partnerships and public publication remain within existing owner/LEDGATo gates.

These rules do not mandate a new score, database, agent, UI, filing, financial commitment or publication. They do not change existing product boundaries, runtime permissions, release topology or merge/production gates.

# ASHWOOD Sponsorship Prospect Research

This queue is for prospective sponsors of **TK's existing independent work**. It is not a client-services lead list.

A prospect qualifies only when there is a credible reason they may want to help a specific ASHWOOD project, experiment, release, research step, or build milestone exist.

## Keep lanes separate

- ASHWOOD sponsorship: fund work already in motion.
- ALVIRA Design Partner / POC: organization tests ALVIRA against its own workflow.
- Modeling: WHO Management.
- Commissioned/client creative work: not part of the current sponsorship offer.

## Qualification gate

Before READY:
- name the existing ASHWOOD project/milestone they could sponsor;
- explain why this sponsor specifically may care about that work;
- verify that sponsorship/grant/creator-support/community/innovation funding is plausible for them;
- verify a real contact route;
- define what their support would unlock;
- do not promise reach, impressions, ownership, favorable coverage, or creative control;
- do not turn the pitch into an offer to build the sponsor's project.

## Record fields

prospect · category · sponsored project/milestone · fit thesis · named contact · verified route · support hypothesis · outreach date · follow-up date · status · response · next action · source

Statuses: research / ready / contacted / replied / meeting / proposal / won / lost.

## Research priorities

Research should start from **current ASHWOOD work needing a bounded resource**, then find aligned funders. Do not start with a generic company list.

Potential sponsor classes:
- arts/culture grants and artist-support programs for creative releases;
- technology/AI ecosystem programs that fund independent experiments or open/public technical work;
- fashion/culture organizations aligned with an existing visual project;
- music/art organizations supporting independent releases;
- local Los Angeles creative and innovation programs.

Every outbound message should name the work being sponsored.

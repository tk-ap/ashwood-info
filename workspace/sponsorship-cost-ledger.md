## Funding rule

A sponsorship offer is READY only when all five are known:

1. existing project;
2. bounded next milestone;
3. external/incremental expense;
4. exact amount or vendor quote;
5. evidence that closes the loop.

Until those fields are complete, Workspace should show the opportunity as **research**, not ready for outbound.

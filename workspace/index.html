Self · operating context

          
Where am I, and what am I optimizing for?

        

        
Reflection informs decisions. Observed evidence remains distinct from interpretation.

      


      

        

          
Compass

          
What does success mean right now?

          
Keep the current definition of success visible enough to shape Today, Work, and Build. Update it when evidence changes—not just when the mood changes.

          
Use: current chapter · priorities · non-negotiables · practical constraints · what “enough” looks like · what is explicitly not the focus.

        


        

          
Thesis → evidence → paths

          
What do I believe about where I am going?

          
Thesis: the current working belief about the next chapter.

          
Evidence: observed facts that support, contradict, or weaken it.

          
Paths: realistic routes available from here, including the cost, dependency, and next test for each.

        


        

          
Decision tool

          
Compare the reality—not the fantasy.

          
Evaluate opportunities against the life and work you are actually trying to build rather than generic prestige or possibility.

          
Check: income/runway · learning · autonomy · proximity to the thesis · time/energy cost · reversibility · evidence created · downside.

        


        

          
Weekly direction / runway

          
What has to be true this week?

          
Connect personal direction to practical constraints. Weekly priorities should feed Today; runway constraints should be allowed to outrank interesting work when necessary.

          
Track: this week’s outcome · commitments · runway pressure · blocked decisions · what to stop · next review.

        


        

          
Coherence across frameworks

          
Different languages. The same life to live.

          
Self can hold multiple ways of describing the same person—values, lived history, personality frameworks, creative identity, spiritual or symbolic systems, career evidence, and practical constraints—without pretending they have equal evidentiary status.

          
Rule: look for recurring patterns across independent lenses, translate them into plain-language hypotheses, then test those hypotheses against lived behavior and outcomes. Convergence can prompt reflection; it does not turn interpretation into fact.

        


        

          
Durable commitments

          
Build the life that can carry who you are.

          
Separate enduring commitments from temporary goals. The system should remember what repeatedly matters across chapters, while allowing the expression of those commitments to change.

          
Trace: recurring value → current expression → supporting evidence → tension/contradiction → next experiment.

        


        

          
Next chapter

          
What appears to be changing?

          
Record what seems to be ending, emerging, or becoming more important, alongside the evidence behind that interpretation. Treat this as a working reflection, not a prediction.

        

      


      

        Reference library · personal frameworks
        

          
Keep longer reflection documents, quick-reference material, and interpretive frameworks here. Astrology, Human Design, Tree of Life, or similar systems may be useful as reflective lenses, but they should remain clearly separated from observed evidence and factual claims.
